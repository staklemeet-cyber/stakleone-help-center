StakleOne Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 23 October 2026

StakleOne is a professional networking and skill-sharing platform. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and the sexual exploitation of children.

Prohibited content and behaviour

Users must not create, upload, request, share, promote or distribute child sexual abuse material, sexually exploit or groom children, or use StakleOne to facilitate child abuse or exploitation. Such conduct is prohibited and may result in content removal, account restrictions or account termination, as appropriate.

Reporting child-safety concerns

Users can report safety concerns through the reporting feature available in the StakleOne app. Reports about suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation can also be sent to staklemeet@gmail.com.

When reporting by email, include relevant details such as the username or content involved, if safe to do so. Do not email, download or forward suspected illegal child sexual abuse material.

Review and enforcement

Child-safety reports are to be reviewed by the service operator. Appropriate action may include restricting or removing content, suspending or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where legally required, and escalating concerns to the appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

Compliance and cooperation

StakleOne's operator is responsible for complying with applicable child-safety laws and cooperating with relevant authorities. Reports must be made to the appropriate regional or national authorities as required by applicable law. The operator must maintain and follow procedures to meet these obligations.

Contact

For child-safety concerns or questions about these standards, contact: staklemeet@gmail.com.
