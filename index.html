Account & Login

Sign in using an available login option in the app. Keep access to your sign-in account secure. If you cannot access your account, contact support.

Circle & Swaps

Use Circle to manage professional connections. Skill swaps are intended for direct skill exchange between participants. Agree on the skills, timing and expectations before starting a swap.

Messages & Posts

Share professional updates and communicate respectfully. Do not post unlawful, deceptive, abusive or harmful content. Report content or users that concern you.

Contact Support

Email: staklemeet@gmail.com

Include a brief description of your issue. Do not email your password or verification codes.

Report a Problem

To report a technical issue, abusive behaviour or inappropriate content, email staklemeet@gmail.com. Include relevant details and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.
