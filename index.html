Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Account and login

Use an available sign-in method provided in the StakleOne app. Keep access to your email or linked sign-in account secure. If you experience login problems, contact support from your registered email address where possible.

Your profile

Your profile may contain your name, username, professional information, biography, city, skills, interests and profile image. Only include information you are comfortable sharing according to the app's visibility settings.

Circle and connections

Use Circle and connection features to build professional relationships. Send requests respectfully and accept requests only when you are comfortable connecting with the person.

Skill swaps

StakleOne supports direct skill exchange between participants. Discuss the skills, scope, schedule and expectations before agreeing to a swap. Both participants should agree voluntarily. Do not assume a swap is guaranteed or that StakleOne guarantees another participant's qualifications, availability or performance.

Posts and messages

Share professional updates and communicate respectfully. Do not post confidential information, passwords, verification codes or another person's personal information without appropriate permission. Use available reporting and blocking features if you encounter abusive or inappropriate behaviour.

Account problems

For technical problems, account access concerns, privacy questions or reports, contact staklemeet@gmail.com. Include a brief explanation and relevant details, but avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

Community Guidelines and Safety

StakleOne is intended to support professional networking and constructive skill sharing. Every user is expected to treat others with respect.

Expected behaviour

  • Communicate respectfully and professionally.
  • Represent your identity, experience and skills honestly.
  • Respect personal boundaries and other users' privacy.
  • Agree clearly on the scope and expectations of skill swaps.
  • Share only content you have the right to publish.
  • Report content or conduct that appears abusive, fraudulent or unsafe.

Prohibited behaviour

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, stalking or intimidation.
  • Fraud, impersonation, scams or deliberate misrepresentation.
  • Unlawful, sexually exploitative or otherwise prohibited content.
  • Hate, discriminatory abuse or encouragement of violence.
  • Sharing private information without proper authorisation.
  • Spam, malicious links, unauthorised access or platform manipulation.
  • Misusing another user's account, content or identity.

Reporting, blocking and enforcement

Use in-app reporting or blocking controls where available. You may also report a concern to staklemeet@gmail.com. Include the relevant username, post or conversation details when safe and appropriate.

Reports may be reviewed and may result in content removal, restrictions, suspension or account termination, depending on the circumstances and applicable law. Do not use reporting tools to harass other users or submit knowingly false reports.

These guidelines describe the expected standard. The app's reporting, blocking and moderation mechanisms must be implemented and operational wherever required by applicable platform policies.
